# Earthy Player

A calm, lightweight desk companion that shows slow Ken Burns-style photo slideshows.  
Designed as a quiet visual alternative to scrolling when you need a short break.

## Features
- Slow zoom-pan (Ken Burns) effect
- Personal image uploads that stay on your device
- Desk Mode (hides controls + keeps the screen awake)
- Optional audio
- Break timer
- AI prompt helper for generating calm images
- Fully client-side — no account required

## How to use
1. Open the page.
2. Click **Controls** and add your own images to a category.
3. Turn on **Desk Mode**.
4. Leave it on your desk.

## Photo Bundles
Curated photography bundles (view-only inside the player) are available separately.  
The player itself remains free.
